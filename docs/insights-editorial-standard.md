# PLUCO Insights editorial standard

PLUCO Insights is a bilingual, source-led private-client knowledge platform. Its goal is qualified discovery and trust, not page count.

## Publication gate

No planned topic becomes public or indexable until it has:

1. A distinct search intent and no material cannibalisation with an existing service page or guide.
2. Current primary sources for legal, immigration, banking, tax or procedural claims.
3. An English edition and a naturally written Persian edition with reciprocal hreflang.
4. A factual review date and a clear update owner.
5. Regulated-claims review, including removal of guarantees and unsupported eligibility statements.
6. A distinct original image, English/Persian alt text and a social crop.
7. Service-relevant internal links, related reading and a restrained call to action.
8. Article, breadcrumb, canonical and social metadata validation.

## Voice

- Calm, discreet, intelligent and precise.
- Premium without conspicuous consumption.
- Aspirational without implying guaranteed outcomes.
- Particularly attentive to the documentary, banking and cross-border realities faced by Iranian clients.
- Lifestyle is the human objective; visas, residence cards, property, companies and banking arrangements are tools.

## Scale policy

The 500-topic file is a private planning inventory, not a permission to auto-publish. Expansion should proceed cluster by cluster after performance and cannibalisation review. Thin, templated and doorway pages remain non-indexable.
